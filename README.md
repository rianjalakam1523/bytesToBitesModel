# bytesToBitesModel

Author: Rian Jalakam
Under the direction of Kevin T. Lee, PHD, this model is a 2-semester (if not longer) long initiative in creating a model capable of Computer Vision in order to scan inventory and perform various applications, from supply chain management to analytics. All the code listed here has been pushed through Git hosted on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano and was coded by Rian Jalakam. Code made by other team members will respectively credit code where it is due, whether it be me or another team member. All code is marked by the Author. 

The setup of the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano was done by Author Rian Jalakam. Being a relatively new initiative launched by me in close collaboration with my professor and the collective cohort of the Initiative for Electrified & Autonomous Mobilty, this will be subject to change in later semesters as more people join and involve themselves in the team upon my departure.

In due time, ownership will transfer to the EPICS Bytes to Bites team to serve as a repository for their coding needs. Being a new initiative, this has yet to be set up and will likely happen within the semester of Spring 2027. 

While the central core of the project revolves around the implementation of AI into the Food Bank ecosystem without leaning into complete replacement, the exact nature of this project is very volatile, leaning into community service (primarily), research, and many other sub-projects branching off of this in order to meet the ever-changing needs of our community partners.
